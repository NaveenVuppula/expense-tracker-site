Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker by Naveen Vuppula

Effective: July 3, 2026

👋 Overview

Expense Tracker ("the App") is developed and operated by Naveen Vuppula. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

🛡️ We believe in privacy by design. We collect only what's necessary to provide you with a great expense tracking experience. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads.

📋 Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — used as your account identifier for login and password recovery
  • Name — used to personalize your experience within the app
  • Profile picture (optional) — uploaded by you to personalize your profile

Financial Data You Enter

  • Expense records — amounts, descriptions, dates, and categories you create
  • Budget settings — monthly budget amounts per category
  • Categories — custom categories you create or modify
  • Receipt images — photos you scan for automatic expense entry (processed by Google Cloud Vision API for text extraction, not stored permanently)
  • Household membership — household name, member relationships, and join codes when you create or join a household

Automatically Collected

  • Device locale/region — to auto-detect your preferred currency
  • Authentication tokens — to keep you securely logged in

🔧 How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the service — store and display your expenses, budgets, and analytics
  • Authentication — verify your identity and secure your account
  • Receipt scanning — extract text from receipt photos to auto-fill expense details
  • Currency detection — display amounts in your local currency
  • Data export — generate CSV and PDF files of your expense data when you request it
  • Household sharing — share expense data with members of your household so you can track spending together

✅ We do NOT use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics tracking, or any purpose beyond providing you with the app's features.

🔐 Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Amazon Web Services (AWS):

  • Authentication — managed by AWS Cognito with industry-standard encryption
  • Expense data — stored in AWS DynamoDB with encryption at rest
  • Profile pictures — stored in AWS S3 with server-side encryption
  • All data transmission — encrypted via HTTPS/TLS

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

🤝 Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — cloud infrastructure, authentication (Cognito), database (DynamoDB), and file storage (S3)
  • Google Cloud Vision API — processes receipt images for text extraction (images are sent to Google's servers for processing but are not stored by Google)
  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication method
  • Apple Sign-In — optional authentication method

Each third-party service operates under their own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their respective policies.

🌍 Data Sharing

🚫 We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Ever.

Your data may only be disclosed:

  • To comply with legal obligations or valid legal processes
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (you would be notified)

🏠 Household Data Sharing

BudgetPal allows you to create or join a household to track shared expenses with family members or roommates. When you participate in a household:

  • Your name and profile picture are visible to other household members
  • Your expenses are shared with household members for combined summaries and analytics
  • Household admin can remove members from the household
  • Join codes are generated to invite new members — these expire and can be regenerated by the admin

✅ Household sharing is entirely opt-in. You must actively create or join a household. You can leave a household at any time, and your expenses will no longer be visible to other members.

All household members must use the same currency. Expense data shared within a household is only visible to members of that household and is never shared outside of it.

🎛️ Your Rights & Choices

  • Access your data — view all your expenses, categories, and budgets within the app at any time
  • Export your data — download your expense data as a CSV file from the app
  • Update your data — edit or delete any expense, category, or budget within the app
  • Leave a household — stop sharing your expenses with household members at any time
  • Delete your account — permanently delete your account and all associated data from within the app (Settings → Delete Account)
  • Change authentication — manage your sign-in method through the app

⚠️ Account deletion is permanent. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
